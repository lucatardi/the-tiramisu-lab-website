Privacy

How we use your details

The short version: we keep what we need to make your order and run your tiramisù card, and you can ask us to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your name and WhatsApp number, which you type on the order form.
  • Your email address, which you enter on the payment page.
  • Your orders: what you ordered, when and where you collected, and what you paid.
  • Your tiramisù card: how many tiramisù you’ve bought, and any free-tiramisù codes you’ve earned.
  • Gift cards: the buyer’s email, plus the names and message written on the card.

We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe.

Why we keep it

  • To make your order and hand it to the right person.
  • To send your confirmation, collection details and a reminder on the day, by email and WhatsApp.
  • To run the tiramisù card: buy 10 tiramisù and your next one is free.
  • To keep the sales records a business is required to keep.

We don’t sell your details or pass them to anyone for advertising.

Who helps us handle it

  • Stripe takes the payment.
  • Cloudflare hosts the ordering system and stores the order records.
  • Resend delivers our emails.
  • WhatsApp carries the messages we send you.

This site has no advertising or tracking cookies. To save you retyping, your browser remembers your name and number on your own device; clearing this site’s data in your browser removes them. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How long we keep it

  • Your tiramisù card and order history: for as long as you’re a customer, or until you ask us to remove them.
  • The details of an upcoming order: until a week after you collect it.
  • Payment and sales records: for as long as tax law requires, which in Ireland is six years.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, or to remove it. Removing it deletes your tiramisù card and order history, including any tiramisù you’ve collected towards a free one. We keep only the payment records the law requires.

To ask, message us on WhatsApp or email info@thetiramisulab.ie. If you’re not happy with how we’ve handled your details, you can also contact the Data Protection Commission.
