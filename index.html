About us

About The Tiramisu Lab

We make high-quality artisan tiramisu from scratch. We operate as a cloud kitchen — no shopfront and no physical point of sale — so every tiramisu is made to order, and never pre-packed or bought in.

Because you order ahead, we collect at two points across Dublin: one in the city centre by day, and one in Clongriffin in the evening. Just pick your flavours and a collection slot, and we’ll meet you there.

Top-quality ingredients

Only the good stuff goes in

A tiramisu is only as good as what’s in it. We don’t cut corners on a single layer — here’s what makes ours different.

Moka-brewed coffee

We brew our coffee the traditional Italian way — in a stovetop moka pot — for a rich, authentic espresso that soaks right through every ladyfinger.

Free-range eggs

Turns out happy chickens make a richer custard.

Irish double cream

We use rich Irish double cream — fresh, local, and the reason every spoonful tastes so indulgently smooth.

Homemade savoiardi

Our ladyfinger biscuits are baked in-house — light and just-sweet, sturdy enough to soak up the coffee without going to mush.

No gelatine, no alcohol

Set with time and technique, not gelatine — and never a drop of alcohol. Just clean, honest ingredients everyone can enjoy.

Homemade mascarpone

We make our mascarpone from scratch, so the cream is silky and rich — no stabilisers, no aftertaste, just the real thing.
